SpottedWise · Account deletion

Delete your SpottedWise account

SpottedWise is a UK app for finding and sharing local deals, run by Ionut Munteanu, a sole trader. You can delete your account at any time.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open SpottedWise and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account…, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

Without the app

Email contact@ionutcm.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject Delete my SpottedWise account.

We only act on requests sent from the account's own email address, so nobody else can delete your account. We delete it in the same way as the app does, and reply to confirm within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, sign-in details and nickname.
  • Your 18+ confirmation and Terms acceptance.
  • Offers you saved to your account.
  • Deals and new places you sent that were not published (waiting for review or rejected).
  • Your "still on?" answers, reports, suggested corrections, nickname reports and requests to look at a decision again.
  • Private notes a moderator wrote to you about your deals.

What is kept, without your name

  • Deals you shared that are already public stay listed for the community, with no name or account attached.
  • Corrections to a place's details that you suggested stay, with no account attached.
  • Moderation records (what was decided, why and when) are kept so decisions can be accounted for, with your nickname removed. They are deleted after 2 years.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers for a limited time. Our providers delete them under their own policies, usually within days.

On your phone

Deleting your account doesn't remove what the app stored on your phone, such as settings or your device's saved list. To remove it, use Settings › Clear data on this device, or uninstall the app.

Exceptions

The account of the person who runs SpottedWise, and the last remaining moderator, can't be deleted in the app, so the service always has someone responsible for it. Moderators can delete their accounts once another moderator exists.
