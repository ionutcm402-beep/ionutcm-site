Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · Applies to SpottedWise for Android and iPhone

This policy explains what personal information the SpottedWise app for Android and iPhone uses, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and your rights. SpottedWise helps people find and share local deals in the UK. It is free. It may show a small, non-personalised advert from Google on a deal's page, which an optional yearly subscription removes. We never sell your information or share it with anyone for their advertising.

Your location

  • Location is optional. You can search by town or postcode instead, without giving location permission.
  • Browsing near you: if you choose "Use my current location" for Offers, your phone asks for permission first. While that choice is on, the app finds your position each time it starts. It never uses your location in the background.
  • Adding a deal: "Use my current location" there is a one-off lookup to list places near you and suggest their postcode. It's worked out on your phone from information built into the app: your position isn't sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't kept. A business is never positioned where your phone is.
  • Your exact position stays on your device, where distances are worked out. To find nearby deals, the app sends our server an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km. It is not stored in our database, but it is not anonymous, and it may appear for a limited time in our hosting providers' technical logs.

What other people can see

Published deals and places are public, together with the nickname of the person who shared them. Your email address, location and reports are never shown to other users. Moderators can see reports, contributions and moderation records so they can review them.

What stays on your device

The app stores some information on your phone so it works quickly and offline: your settings, unsent drafts, saved offers, people whose deals you chose to hide, and recently loaded deals. This is not used for tracking. Signing out removes your sign-in and recently loaded deals. "Clear data on this device" in Settings removes the rest but keeps you signed in. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Scanning a leaflet or menu

If you use "Scan a leaflet or menu" when adding a deal, your phone reads the words on the photo using Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your phone through Google Play services. The photo isn't sent to us or to Google, it isn't kept by the app and it isn't added to the deal: only the words it finds are put into the form for you to check. To keep the reader working, ML Kit sends Google technical information such as your phone's model and Android version, the app's name and version, and how well the reading worked; it doesn't include the photo or its words.

Information about businesses

SpottedWise shows businesses' names, types, addresses and positions so people can find deals. This information comes from members, from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) and from the Food Standards Agency's food hygiene ratings. Where a business is run by a person, for example a sole trader, some of it may be personal information. We use it because of our legitimate interests in helping people find local deals, and we show only business details. A business that seems to be run from a home is shown only by its postcode area, never at the house. If you run a business and want its details corrected or removed, email contact@ionutcm.com.

Service providers

We use these providers to run SpottedWise. They process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd.): our database and sign-in. Your information is stored on its servers in London, UK, and it processes it only on our behalf, under its data processing agreement.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., trading as Resend, USA): sends our sign-in emails. It receives your email address and the email it delivers, and keeps a record of sent emails for a limited time. Our emails are sent from its Ireland (EU) region; its main operations are in the United States.
  • OpenFreeMap (Hyperknot Software Kft., Hungary): map images. When you open the map, your device requests map images from it, which reveals your IP address to it and to Cloudflare, which delivers them. OpenFreeMap deletes its server logs after 7 days.

Your information is stored in the UK. Our providers may access it from other countries to run and support their service: Supabase is based in Singapore, Resend in the United States, and OpenFreeMap in Hungary (EU). Where personal information leaves the UK, it goes either to a country the UK recognises as protecting it adequately (such as EU countries), or under the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers: the ICO's International Data Transfer Addendum, which is part of Supabase's and Resend's data processing agreements, and, for Resend, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which it is certified.

Adverts

  • A small advert may appear at the bottom of a deal's page. It's provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited).
  • Adverts are never personalised: Google chooses them from the page and general context, such as the approximate area worked out from your internet address, not from a profile of you.
  • To show an advert, your device sends Google technical information: your IP address, device type and settings, and information Google uses to count adverts, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud. Google may store or read information on your device for this, which is why we ask for your consent first where the law requires it. Adverts are limited to content suitable for most audiences. Google is an independent controller of this information under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and may process it outside the UK under its own safeguards for international transfers.
  • We never give Google your location, email, nickname, account or anything you contribute.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad choices. If you don't consent, Google may still show adverts in a more limited way, or none; the app works the same.

No ads subscription

If you subscribe to remove adverts, Google Play or the App Store takes your payment, under its own privacy policy as an independent controller. We don't receive your card details, name or email address from them. The app asks the store, on your phone, whether your subscription is active so it can switch adverts off; this isn't sent to us. If you contact us about your subscription, we use what you send only to answer you.

Legal basis

The law lets us use your information only for a valid reason. These are ours:

  • Providing the service you asked for (contract): your account and sign-in, the deals, places and corrections you send, your "still on?" answers, and offers you save.
  • Your location, only when you choose to use it (contract): to show deals near you, or nearby places when you add a deal. You can turn it off at any time and search by town or postcode instead.
  • Our legitimate interests in running a safe, honest community: checking you are 18 or over, reviewing deals, handling reports and requests to look at a decision again, keeping moderation records, and protecting the service from abuse (including technical logs). We have considered your rights and keep only what these purposes need.
  • Your consent: for Google to store or read information on your device to show adverts, where the law requires it. You can withdraw it in Settings › Ad choices.
  • Our legitimate interests in funding a free service: showing non-personalised adverts.
  • Legal obligation: where a law requires us to keep or share information, for example to answer a lawful request from the authorities.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, email address and nickname: until you delete your account.
  • Deals you shared that are public: they stay public after you delete your account, without your name.
  • Deals you sent that were not published: 90 days, then deleted.
  • "Still on?" answers, reports and suggested corrections, once dealt with: 1 year, then deleted.
  • Requests to look at a decision again, once decided: 1 year, then deleted.
  • Expired or archived deals: 2 years, then deleted.
  • Moderation records (what was decided, why and when): 2 years, then deleted. If you delete your account, your nickname is removed from them straight away.
  • Sign-ups that were never finished (a code was requested but never entered): 30 days, then deleted.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting providers for a short time under their own policies, then deleted by them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings › Account › Delete my account. If you can't use the app, see how to request account deletion.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for information you gave us in a portable format. Email contact@ionutcm.com from the address linked to your account. To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from that address. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first: email contact@ionutcm.com and we'll acknowledge it within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Age

You must be 18 or over to create an account and contribute. Browsing deals does not need an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we make an important change, for example to what we collect or who we share it with, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
