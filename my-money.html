What it does

  • Calendar of shifts, split shifts, overtime, holidays and sick days, for one or more jobs
  • Custom pay periods and paydays, with gross and estimated take-home pay
  • Holiday allowance, payments and recurring payments, and a wallet for cash and card
  • Optional app lock with a PIN, fingerprint or face
  • Backups you save and keep yourself

Free and Pro

My Money is free. My Money Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited jobs and advanced pay adjustments. On Android it also removes the single banner advert on the Overview screen. The iPhone app has no adverts.
