About

Technology should make everyday life easier.

I'm an independent app developer based in the UK. I build focused apps that solve everyday problems, without the features nobody asked for.

ionutcm is one person, not a company with departments. I design, build and support every app myself, and I answer every email myself too.

My apps keep your information on your device, work without an account, and try to be clear about what they do. If something doesn't work the way you expect, I'd like to hear about it.

What I care about.

  • Simplicity

    Each app does one job, and does it without fuss.

  • Privacy

    Your information stays on your phone. No accounts, no servers holding your data.

  • Practical usefulness

    Every feature has to earn its place by solving a real, everyday problem.

  • Clean design

    Calm screens, clear numbers and nothing competing for your attention.

  • Independence

    No investors and no growth targets. Just apps I'd want to use myself.
