Habit Dial

Small habits.
Clear progress.

Habit Dial turns each month into a single dial. Every habit is a ring, every day is a segment, and a glance shows how the month is going. It works offline, with nothing to sign up for.

How it works

Step 1

Set up your habits

Add up to eight daily habits for the month and give each one a colour. Each habit becomes a ring on the dial.

Step 2

Mark today's progress

Tap a habit when you've done it. The matching segment on the dial fills in straight away.

Step 3

See the month at a glance

The dial shows every day of the month, so streaks and gaps are easy to spot without charts or scores.

Thoughtful details.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your phone's appearance, or choose one yourself.

  • Reminders

    An optional reminder for each habit, on the days you choose. Skipped once you have ticked it.

  • Optional App Lock

    A 4- or 6-digit PIN, with fingerprint or face unlock. Habit Dial is hidden in the recent-apps screen while it's on.

  • Backups you keep

    Create a backup file yourself and restore it when you need to.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account, no server, no cloud sync, no adverts and no analytics. Your habits are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app. Reminders are created and shown on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Read the privacy policy

Availability.

Free to download, with a three-day trial of all eight habits. One habit stays free forever; a one-time lifetime unlock adds all eight. No subscription.
