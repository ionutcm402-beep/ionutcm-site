Everything in its place.

Calendar

Record shifts, hours, holidays and earnings.

Each pay period is a calendar. Tap a day to record a shift, a holiday or a sick day, and see the hours and extra pay on the day itself. Paydays are marked.

Overview

Understand earnings and payments at a glance.

Overview adds up the estimated income from every job, the payments due this month, and what is left over.

Payments

Organise recurring and one-off payments.

Rent, bills and one-off costs live in one list. Tick them off as you pay and see how much is still to go.

Wallet

Track cash and card balances.

Record money in, money out and transfers, and always know what you hold in cash and on card.

Holiday allowance

Know how much holiday you have left.

Set your employer's holiday year and allowance, in days or hours. Holidays you add in the Calendar are counted automatically, and you can add adjustments.

Settings and tools

Handy tools, built in.

A calculator, a pay calculator, a payday countdown, a side-by-side job comparison and an hours-needed tool, next to your jobs and pay periods.

Thoughtful details.

  • Shifts your way

    Split shifts, several shifts a day, overtime, holidays and sick days, with extra money on any day.

  • Several jobs, one view

    Each job keeps its own pay rate, pay period and payday. Overview adds them together.

  • Estimated take-home pay

    Gross and estimated take-home pay from your own rates and deduction setting.

  • Optional app lock

    A 4- or 6-digit PIN, with fingerprint, face or Face ID, locking straight away or after 1 or 5 minutes.

  • Backups you keep

    Save a backup file wherever you like and restore it on a new phone.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account, no server and no bank connection. Your records are stored only on your phone and never sent to us, and your app lock PIN itself is never stored. The free Android version shows one advert from Google on the Overview screen, with consent asked first in the UK and EEA; My Money Pro removes it. The iPhone app has no adverts.

Read the privacy policy

Availability.

Free to download. My Money Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited jobs and advanced pay adjustments, and removes the advert on Android. No subscription.
