Everything in its place.

Calendar

Record shifts, hours, holidays and earnings.

Each pay period is a calendar. Tap a day to record a shift, a holiday or a sick day, and see the hours and extra pay on the day itself. Paydays are marked.

Overview

Understand earnings and payments at a glance.

Overview adds up the estimated income from every job, the payments due this month, and what is left over.

Payments

Organise recurring and one-off payments.

Rent, bills and one-off costs live in one list. Tick them off as you pay and see how much is still to go.

Wallet

Track cash and card balances.

Record money in, money out and transfers, and always know what you hold in cash and on card.

Thoughtful details.

  • Several jobs, one view

    Each job keeps its own pay rate, pay period and payday. Overview adds them together.

  • Estimated take-home pay

    Gross and estimated take-home pay from your own rates and tax setting.

  • Optional app lock

    Protect the app with a PIN and your phone's fingerprint, face or Face ID.

  • Backups you keep

    Save a backup file wherever you like and restore it on a new phone.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account, no server and no bank connection. Your records are stored only on your phone and never sent to us. The free Android version shows one advert from Google on the Overview screen, with consent asked first in the UK and EEA; My Money Pro removes it. The iPhone app has no adverts.
