SpottedWise

Local deals.
Spotted by locals.

SpottedWise brings local deals into one place: lunch offers, happy hours, things to do, shopping and everyday services. Deals are shared by the community, and people say whether a deal is still on, so you can see when it was last checked.

Everything in its place.

Offers

Deals near you, at a glance.

Choose a town, a postcode or your current location and a distance. Each deal shows the price, when it's on next and when someone last checked it.

Deal details

Everything you need to know.

What's included and what isn't, the conditions, the days and times, and where to find it. Save it for later or share it with a friend.

Add a deal

Spotted one? Share it.

Find the place, then type the offer, or scan a leaflet or menu and your phone fills it in for you to check. Deals you share help everyone nearby save.

Thoughtful details.

  • Checked by the community

    People say whether a deal is still on, so you can see when it was last confirmed.

  • Search your way

    Look by town or postcode, or use your location only if you choose to.

  • Saved deals

    Keep the deals you like in one list. Sign in and they follow you to your other devices.

  • Fair and moderated

    Anyone can report a deal that's wrong, and reports are reviewed. Businesses can ask for corrections.

Privacy

Private by design.

Browsing needs no account. Location is optional: your exact position stays on your phone, and only an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, is used to find nearby deals. Contributors sign in with an email address, which is never shown to anyone. SpottedWise has no analytics of its own. Adverts are non-personalised and come from Google AdMob, which receives some device and usage information; the privacy policy explains what.
