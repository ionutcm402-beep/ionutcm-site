My Money

How do I get My Money Pro back on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google or Apple account you bought it with, open My Money, go to Settings → My Money Pro and tap Restore purchase.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Settings → Backup & restore and save a backup file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Google Drive or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, open the same screen and choose that file.

How do I track my holiday allowance?

Go to Settings → Jobs, choose the job, then Holiday Allowance → Track Allowance. Set when your holiday year starts and your allowance in days or hours. Holidays you add in the Calendar are then counted automatically.

Can you recover my data?

No. Your records are only on your phone, so there is never a copy on our side. Saving a backup regularly is the best protection.

How do I change my advert consent?

In the free Android version, go to Settings → Ad privacy choices. My Money Pro removes adverts completely.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall My Money, or on Android clear its storage in Settings → Apps → My Money → Storage. Delete any backup files you saved as well.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple. Request a refund through Google Play (play.google.com → Order history) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Habit Dial

How do I restore the lifetime unlock on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the account you bought it with. Habit Dial checks your purchase with Google Play when it opens.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

Create a backup file in Habit Dial's Settings, keep it somewhere you can reach from the new phone, then restore it there. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app, so it won't copy your habits by itself.

I forgot my App Lock PIN.

Use fingerprint or face unlock if you turned it on. If not, the only way back in is to clear the app's data in Android settings, which also deletes your habits unless you have a backup.

Why didn’t my reminder arrive?

Check that notifications are on for Habit Dial in your phone’s Settings → Apps → Habit Dial → Notifications, and that battery saving isn’t stopping the app. Reminders can arrive a few minutes late while the phone saves battery. There’s no reminder on a day you’ve already ticked the habit. If you haven’t opened Habit Dial for three weeks, reminders pause until you open it again.

How do I delete all my data?

In Habit Dial, go to Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Request a refund through Google Play (play.google.com → Order history).
